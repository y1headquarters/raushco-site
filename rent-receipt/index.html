Why rent receipts matter for your taxes

Proof your employer will accept

If HRA is part of your salary, payroll needs documented proof you actually paid rent before it can treat that part of your pay as exempt from tax. A properly formatted receipt is that proof.

The ₹1,00,000-a-year PAN rule

Once your total rent for the financial year crosses ₹1,00,000, the Income Tax Department requires your landlord's PAN on the receipts you submit — otherwise the exemption can be denied.

The revenue stamp rule

Under the Indian Stamp Act, a cash rent payment over ₹5,000 needs a revenue stamp affixed to the receipt, signed across by the landlord. Paying by bank transfer, UPI, or cheque skips this entirely.

One form, a whole year of receipts

Most months look identical except for the date. Fill the form once, pick a date range, and this generates every month's receipt as its own page in a single PDF.
