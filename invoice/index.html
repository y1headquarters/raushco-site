Why a proper invoice matters

Faster payment

An invoice with clear line items, a due date, and a total that's easy to check gets paid faster than a casual message or a bank transfer request.

A paper trail for both sides

A dated, numbered invoice is the record you and your client both point to if there's ever a dispute about what was billed or when it was due.

Looks like a real business

A clean, itemized PDF signals you run things properly — which matters just as much for a freelancer's first client as it does for a growing team.

One form, every invoice

Keep the same From details each time and just swap in the client and line items — the invoice number and dates default sensibly so you can move fast.
