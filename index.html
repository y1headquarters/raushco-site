Disclaimers and footnotes

Raushco is a fintech platform, not a bank. Deposit and card products are provided through our partner banks and NBFCs, as applicable, and are governed by each partner's own terms.

  1. Raushco is not a bank and does not itself hold customer deposits. Savings, current, and fixed-deposit balances are held with our partner banks, and DICGC deposit insurance applies only to deposits held with DICGC-member banks, up to the standard per-depositor, per-bank limit; it does not apply to NBFC-issued deposits or to balances held with a partner NBFC. Where a product sweeps funds across more than one partner bank, insurance is calculated separately at each bank, not on the combined balance.

  2. The yield shown for Treasury by Raushco is a blended, indicative rate across the partner banks and NBFCs in the sweep network at the time of publishing, weighted by how a typical balance is allocated across them. It is set by each partner, not by Raushco, and is variable and subject to change without notice; past yield is not a guarantee of future yield, and the rate you actually earn depends on which partners are live for your account, your balance tier, and how long funds stay swept with each partner.

    Funds swept under Treasury are held as deposits at the underlying partner banks, not with Raushco, and DICGC insurance applies at each partner bank up to the standard per-depositor, per-bank limit, calculated separately per bank rather than on the combined swept balance. A sweep allocation to a partner NBFC, where offered, is not a bank deposit and is not DICGC-insured; any such allocation is identified as an NBFC placement before you opt in.

  3. Venture debt and working capital loans available through Raushco are originated and underwritten by our partner NBFC, not by Raushco itself. Working capital loans are unsecured and underwritten against your business's own cash flow; venture debt loan size, tenure, and approval are not guaranteed and depend on the partner NBFC's own eligibility review.
  4. The Raushco Card is issued by our partner bank pursuant to a license from Mastercard; card issuance and credit limits are subject to underwriting by that issuing partner, and eligibility criteria are set by the partner, not by Raushco. Cashback of up to 1.5% on eligible card spend is indicative, applies only to spend categories the issuing partner designates as eligible, and is credited on the schedule the partner publishes; cashback may be reduced, capped, or withdrawn if your account is closed or not in good standing. The FD-backed credit card is secured against a fixed deposit held with the issuing partner; its limit is set as a percentage of that FD's value and is not a guarantee of a higher unsecured limit later.
  5. Real-time transfers over UPI and IMPS depend on the receiving bank and that payment rail being available at the time of transfer; NEFT and RTGS settle on the same business day rather than instantly, and timing for all rails can vary around bank holidays or maintenance windows.
  6. Dedicated relationship managers and capital advisors are available to business accounts on eligible plans and balance tiers; availability varies by plan.
  7. The "11+ partner banks & NBFCs" figure reflects partners integrated with Raushco as of this page's last publish date, across savings, deposits, gold loans, and lending combined; not every partner is available for every product or in every state.
  8. Annual Percentage Yield (APY) of up to 7.00% on personal savings reflects the rate in effect as of this page's last publish date and is offered through our partner bank; it is a variable rate that may change at any time without prior notice.
