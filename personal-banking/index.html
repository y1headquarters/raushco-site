Frequently asked questions

Your Raushco account covers goal tracking, fixed and recurring deposits, gold & silver holdings, bill pay, and the FD-backed credit card — all under one login, with no extra product sign-ups.

The card is secured against a fixed deposit you hold with us. You spend against the card as normal while the underlying deposit keeps accruing — the deposit is what sets your limit.

Deposits booked through our partner banks are eligible for DICGC deposit insurance up to ₹5,00,000 per depositor, per bank — the same coverage that applies to any deposit at that bank.

You can buy gold or silver in small amounts directly inside the app, track its value alongside your other goals, and sell back when you need the liquidity.

There's no fee to open an account, create goals, or hold gold & silver. The FD-backed card has no annual fee.

Encryption in transit and at rest, two-factor authentication on every login, and we never store your full card number on our own servers.

Once logged in, you can reach our support team directly from the app, or write to support@raushco.com.

Disclaimers and footnotes

Raushco is a fintech platform, not a bank. Deposit and card products are provided through our partner banks and NBFCs, as applicable.

  1. "FD-backed credit card" means the card's spending limit is secured against a fixed deposit held with a Raushco partner bank or NBFC; terms vary by partner.
  2. 7.00% is an indicative annual percentage yield; the actual rate depends on tenure and the partner bank or NBFC, and is subject to change.
  3. DICGC deposit insurance applies only to deposits held with DICGC-member banks, up to the standard per-depositor, per-bank limit; it does not apply to NBFC-issued deposits.
  4. Joint and trust account support varies by partner bank or NBFC and may require additional documentation.
  5. Real-time payments (UPI/IMPS) depend on the receiving bank and payment rail being available at the time of transfer.
  6. Automations and prompts act only on rules and permissions you set; Raushco does not move your money without your authorization.
